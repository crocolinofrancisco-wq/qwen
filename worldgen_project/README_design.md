Python program to generate realistic worlds for the simulations.

**World Generation: Main characteristics**
- World made by square tiles, fully configurable resolution.
- Optimization: To optimize, the number of tiles is reduced by unifying similar near tiles. Instead of 50 tiles of ocean, there is only one big tile. Criteria for unifications: similarity in data (e.g precipitations, ground material, temperature, height, etc) and direct connections. Configurable similarity pecentage.
- No biomes: Biomes are defined during the simulation.
- Dynamics simulation of the world, simulating for example plate drift through the time.
- Export in .json format.

**Generation Phase 1**
- Plates: First a map is generated depending on the final resolution (not pixel by pixel but same proportion) wanted, then, in this blank sheet there are drawed a configurable amount of dots in y and x axis, they are not fully evenly distributed but have a certain noise. Then major plates are generated by selecting random points as center and expand to all neareast points (Voronoi), this is also applied for individual points. Finally small plates are generated randomly selecting a group of shapes near themselves that are in plate borders. Plates are also separed into oceanic and continental, oceanic plates have a divider for the heightmap. K.D TREE is implemented for lowering processing times.
- Continents: Heighmap generated with perlin noise, asigning a random point of heighmap to each tectonic plate. The heightmap is smoothed on continents edges for smoother transitions. There are 4 layers of perlin noise composing the heightmap, one more detailed than the other but has an smalles influence on the final heightmap. PD: Perlin noise has a certain limit to not generate mountains.
- Plate Boundaries: As in real life there are different types of boundaries: Divergent, convergent and transform. By assigning a random move direction to each plate the stress level of each boundary can be calculated and then affecting the generation of terrain.
- Climate: It calculated by the lattitude, the closer to the center, the hotter, but its also affected by the height, making heights closer to the ocean level hotter. There is also a configuration for tilt and noise.
- Precipitations and humidity: Start high at the ecuator, then fall and increase again, all in a smooth transitions but the first transition to arid zones is more prominent than the transition from arid to humidity again. Noise is also applyed.
- Continent drift: Depending on the plate moving direction, when simulating geographic evolution, there is a chance for a random points inside a plate to move in that direction, transforming every point in a certain radious area to the plate and then coming back to its original position. Once a point is already selected for moving, close points have a chance to move with it in the same direction.
- Mountains: Their shape vary depending on the boundary type. Perlin noise is used to generate mountains near boundaries, they have 4 octaves of noise for more detail (more deatiled but less influence). They have also have 3 special layer of noise (Fractal Ridge Blending by Devote) which consists in: first you have a function smoothly giving values beetween 0 and 1, then you move shift the whole function in a value beetween 0.35-0.65, after that you flip every value below zero (opposite, multiply by negative), finally you flip the whole function and adjust it to fit beetween 0-1 again. By this method we get sharp peaks. To finish the perlin noise layers and fractal ridge noise layers are smoothly blended between.
- Rivers: First random points in ocean coasts are selected, this are the river mouth, there must be a large radius detector to avoid creating river mouth on small ponds. Then random points are generated in mountains, that are where rivers will born, from this point the generation of the flow points is started by a radius that has 10 possible points in its circumference, the selected dot is selected by weight of its height and direction to the mouth (lower heights have more weight and when the heighs are relatively similar direction gains more weight), this is repeated until the radius in smaller than the mouth point, once the flow reachs there the points are connected. The width of the river flow is determined by the previous doth width and plus the local precipitation average level. If there is another river point near it merges, this has to be also took into account when first calculating rivers. There is a small chance for a river to split at some point of its flow, sometimes spliting and merging again forming islands, but at the end points there is a larger chance for splitting to simulate deltas.
- Spheric world: The world isnt displayed as a sphere but it real is so the generation has to take that into account because for example, a tectonic plate can continue in the other edge of the map. The first phase should be donde in an sphere an then proyected into a flat map.
- Dinamic world: The user can select the number of years they want to simulate, the simulator generates a different map every specified by the user time of years, at the exportation, all generated phases of the dynamic are exported as different maps.

**Generation Phase 2**
- Tile convertion: Once the map is fully generated it is converted to a custom resolution tilemap by averaging the colours on every tile and choosing the most frequent (excepting rivers, a tile that contains a bit of rivers automatically considered full river to avoid unconnected rivers)
- Optimization: Tiles are unifyed in groups by similarity and nearness.
- Stats: Every tile receives its stats depending on the original map.
- Export: The data is converted and exported.

**UI**
- The program has an intuitive UI to fully customize and watch the live process.
- Isometric 3d visor to see heights of the original map and the tilemap.
